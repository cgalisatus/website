peacock
sometimes, and just sometimes, everything feels like magic; there’s a peacock that lives on my street, bright green and deceptively large tropical parrots that nest in the tree in my yard - in los angeles, california - and tristan and i watched the same sunset as miranda over facetime. everyone’s taking pictures of the sunsets but we’ve been spoiled with stupidly stunning sunsets this week thanks to the realest most mythical storm there ever was, with a counterpart that is unironically named la niña - i hope we never stop posting them on our ig stories so long as ig (derogatory) hasn’t yet fully crumbled. i’m in disbelief at what AI can do these days but i believe it more swiftly and easefully than i believe that we get to see the sky like this. we’re all getting eaten alive by mosquitos but we all are, mosquitos love him or whatever but they also love her. there’s an amplified mariachi band playing down the street, its sound wafting through my windows like the scent of my neighbor doing laundry. each window i stick my head out of, it sounds like it’s coming from another one, the music i mean. last week i got so used to seeing peacocks in unexpected locations that on first glance i believed the man on the roof to be a peacock. i also went into cat mode, hanging with andi while bradley was away, that on first glance i believed the dog to be a horse walking down meridian. all i want is the bravery to tell everyone exactly how i feel
